Your room should feel like your safe space. Does it?

Open the window and the dust and mosquitoes come in. Close it and the room feels stuffy. Most of us just put up with it.

Outside air brings the outside in

Dust and pollution from busy streets and construction drift in whenever the window is open, and settle on everything you own.

Fresh air, plus mosquitoes

A breeze at night often comes with uninvited guests, and a disturbed sleep.

Bulky devices stay where they are

Most purifiers need a socket all day and are awkward to move between rooms, let alone between flats.

Introducing ClimaPod

Meet ClimaPod. Small Device. Big Difference.

ClimaPod sits in the gap of your window. It draws in outdoor air, passes it through a mosquito mesh and multi-stage filters, and releases it into your room. One compact device for fresh air and insect protection, running on a rechargeable battery.

  • Fresh air, filtered firstUnlike purifiers that recirculate room air, ClimaPod is designed to bring in filtered outdoor air.
  • Mosquito mesh built inKeep the window working for you without letting insects through the gap.
  • Battery-powered and tool-freeNo permanent installation, so it suits hostels, PGs and rented flats.
See how it works

Six things, one compact device.

Every benefit below is a design goal for the prototype. Where a number appears, it's a target we still have to prove.

Cleaner incoming air

A pre-filter, a proposed HEPA layer and activated carbon work in sequence on the air coming in.

Fresh-air ventilation

A DC blower draws outdoor air in, so the room isn't just recycling the same stale air.

Mosquito protection

An integrated mesh is designed to stop mosquitoes and other insects at the window.

Portable design

Lift it by the handle and move it to another room or your next flat.

Target 1.5–2 kg

Rechargeable power

Runs without being tethered to a socket, which also helps during power cuts.

Target 3–4 hours

Temperature and humidity

A built-in display shows indoor temperature and humidity at a glance.

Follow the air.

Seven stages from the street to your room. Select any stage, or let the air walk you through it.

1

Outdoor air

Air is drawn in from outside through the window-facing side of the device, the part that sits in your window gap.

These filtration stages are ClimaPod's proposed product architecture. Materials, order and performance will be confirmed through prototype testing.

Designed for modern Indian living.

Whether it's a shared hostel room, a PG near campus or a flat you'll leave in eleven months, your room still deserves fresh air. ClimaPod is built for homes you don't own and can't drill into.

Hostel and PG students

Small shared rooms, no say over the fittings, and a budget to respect. ClimaPod fits the window you already have.

"I can't install anything here, but I still need to open the window."

People in rented apartments

No drilling, no landlord permission, no deposit deductions. When you move, it goes in the box with everything else.

"Whatever I buy has to move with me."

Urban households

Families who want fresh air in a bedroom or study without dust and mosquitoes, and without another permanent fixture.

"We want the window open for the kids' room, minus the mosquitoes."

Quotes are illustrative examples of the needs we're designing for, not customer testimonials.
