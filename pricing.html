Cleaner Air. Smarter Living. Within Reach.

One price for five jobs: filtration, fresh-air ventilation, mosquito protection, portability and battery power. Here's what we're aiming for and how we got there.

Proposed introductory price

₹4,999

Indicative student-project price, subject to prototype validation. Not a confirmed retail price.

  • ClimaPod unit with mesh, pre-filter, HEPA and carbon filters
  • Built-in rechargeable battery
  • Adjustable window-fit panel
  • Temperature and humidity display

Proposed package, subject to change.

No pre-orders or payments are being taken.

What the price is designed to cover.

ClimaPod combines jobs that usually need separate products. The price reflects the combination, not any one feature.

Air filtration

Pre-filter, proposed HEPA and activated carbon in one cartridge stack.

Fresh-air ventilation

A DC blower brings outdoor air in rather than recirculating room air.

Mosquito protection

An integrated mesh at the intake, so the window can stay useful.

Portability

No installation, a carry handle and a 1.5–2 kg target weight.

Battery operation

Runs without a socket, with a 3–4 hour target per charge.

Accessible, not cheap: the target is a price a student or tenant can plan for, while still paying for real filtration, a proper battery and a build that lasts through moves.

Questions about price.

More answers on the product FAQ.

Is ₹4,999 the final price?

No. It's an indicative maximum for this student project, subject to prototype validation. We'll update it openly if it changes.

Can I pre-order or pay now?

No. We aren't taking pre-orders or payments. The waitlist only collects your details so we can share project updates.

Will there be ongoing costs?

Most likely replacement filters and the electricity used for charging. We'll publish filter costs and intervals once testing shows how long they last.
