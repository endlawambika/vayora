Fresh air in. Dust and mosquitoes kept out.

ClimaPod is a compact, battery-powered unit that fits into the gap of a sliding window. It draws outdoor air through a mosquito mesh and multi-stage filters, then releases it into your room. No drilling, no permanent installation.

ClimaPod is designed to bring filtered outdoor air indoors. It is not an air conditioner or air cooler.

It does not cool your room, and we won't claim it can unless future prototype testing shows otherwise. Its job is filtered fresh-air ventilation and mosquito protection.

Advanced version

Take it apart, layer by layer.

Air travels from left to right, outdoors to indoors. Select a component to highlight it and read what it does.

  1. A two-part shell. The outdoor side has a louvred grille intended to protect the filters from larger debris; the room side carries the display, controls and vents.
  2. The first barrier at the intake, a fine integrated mesh designed to stop mosquitoes and other insects from coming through the window gap.
  3. A coarse layer for larger dust, lint and hair, intended to extend the life of the finer filters behind it.
  4. A pleated HEPA layer for fine airborne particles. We don't state an efficiency figure: it will be measured in prototype testing.
  5. A carbon layer designed to reduce certain odours, such as cooking smells or smoke drifting in from outside.
  6. The fan that pulls air through every layer and into the room. A DC motor suits battery power and allows variable speeds.
  7. Sits low in the body for stability. Target run time is 3–4 hours per charge; actual run time will depend on fan speed and is still to be validated.
  8. Mounted on the room side so they read indoor conditions, shown on the circular display. A PM2.5 sensor is planned only for an advanced version.

Highlights in detail.

The five things people ask about most. Pick one.

Filtration technology

Most indoor purifiers clean the air that's already in your room. ClimaPod is designed to clean the air on its way in.

  • Mesh, then pre-filter, HEPA and activated carbon, each catching something different.
  • Filters are designed to slide out from the room side, so replacing them shouldn't mean removing the unit.
  • Replacement interval and cost will be set after testing.
Proposed architecture

Battery-powered operation

No socket near the window? No problem. ClimaPod is designed to run on its own rechargeable battery.

  • Target run time of 3–4 hours per charge.
  • Designed to keep running through short power cuts.
  • Charging method and charge time will be confirmed with the prototype.
To be validated

Window attachment mechanism

Slide the window open, place ClimaPod in the gap, and extend the adjustable panel to close the rest of the opening.

  • Designed for the sliding windows common in Indian apartments and hostels.
  • Designed as a tension fit with soft seals: no screws, no drilling, nothing left behind.
  • Supported window sizes will be defined during prototype testing.
Proposed design

Portability and easy relocation

A recessed handle and a target weight of 1.5–2 kg mean ClimaPod can follow you from the bedroom to the study, or to your next flat.

  • Designed to be removed and refitted without tools.
  • Small enough to pack with your things when you move.
Target spec

Temperature and humidity monitoring

The circular display shows your room's temperature and relative humidity, so you can see how the room feels, not just guess.

  • Sensors sit on the room side to read indoor conditions.
  • An optional PM2.5 reading is planned for an advanced version.
  • ClimaPod displays temperature; it does not change it.

Who we're designing it for.

Three groups shaped the brief. Each needs fresh air without permanent changes to the room.

Hostel and PG students

Shared rooms with fittings they can't change and a careful budget.

  • Fits the existing window
  • Works without a nearby socket
  • Helps at night with the window open

Tenants in rented apartments

People who move every year or two and can't drill or install.

  • No landlord permission needed
  • Leaves no marks behind
  • Moves with you

Urban households

Families who want one room, a bedroom or study, to feel fresher.

  • Room-by-room use
  • Mosquito protection with the window open
  • Simple, visible readings

Questions, answered honestly.

Where something hasn't been tested yet, we say so.

What is ClimaPod?

ClimaPod is a proposed portable window device that brings filtered outdoor air into your room and keeps mosquitoes out. It's a student product concept currently in the prototype stage.

Is it an air conditioner?

No. ClimaPod is not an air conditioner or air cooler and is not designed to lower room temperature. It provides filtered fresh-air ventilation and mosquito protection. The display shows temperature; it doesn't change it.

How does the filtration system work?

Outdoor air passes through a mosquito mesh, a pre-filter for larger dust, a proposed HEPA filter for fine particles and an activated carbon layer for certain odours. A DC blower then moves the air into your room. Filtration performance hasn't been measured yet.

Specifications under development
Does it bring fresh air from outside?

Yes, that's the core idea. Unlike conventional purifiers that recirculate indoor air, ClimaPod is designed to draw outdoor air in and filter it on the way.

How does the mosquito mesh work?

A fine mesh sits at the air intake, before any filter. It's designed to physically stop mosquitoes and other insects, while the adjustable panel closes the rest of the window gap.

Is it battery-powered?

Yes. ClimaPod is designed around a rechargeable battery so it can run without a socket near the window.

How long does the battery last?

Our target is 3–4 hours per charge. This hasn't been tested yet, and real run time will depend on fan speed and battery age.

Specifications under development
Can it be installed in rented apartments?

That's exactly who it's for. It's designed to fit sliding windows using tension and soft seals, with no drilling or permanent changes. Supported window sizes will be confirmed during prototype testing.

Is the price final?

No. ₹4,999 is an indicative maximum price for this student project, subject to prototype validation and component costs. See the pricing page for what it's designed to include.

When will it be available?

There's no launch date yet. ClimaPod is a concept in development, and availability depends on prototype testing. Join the waitlist and we'll share progress as it happens.

Want to see it tested?

We'll share prototype results, including what doesn't work yet. Join the waitlist to follow along.
