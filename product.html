Four modules inside.

A top-down view of the internal design. Select a part to highlight it and read what it does.

1 · Air purification module

  1. Catches larger dust and hair first. Washable.
  2. A pleated filter designed to help reduce fine airborne particles.
  3. Designed to help reduce certain odours in the airflow.

2 · Cooling module

  1. The tank above keeps the pad damp, so air passing through it is cooled. Fill it from the lid on top.
  2. Moves the main airflow from the rear intake to the front outlet. Separate from the MosquitoGuard fan.
  3. Adjustable front vents that aim the airflow at you.

3 · Electronics and battery module

  1. Temperature and humidity sensing, with the readings and controls on the front display.
  2. Powers every module, so Vayora runs without a cable once charged.
  3. Runs the fans, UV-A light, sensors and comfort modes, and manages charging.

4 · MosquitoGuard module (side)

  1. A replaceable cartridge in the side compartment that helps attract mosquitoes towards the intake.
  2. A low-power UV-A LED beside the side intake, working with the scent lure.
  3. A small, low-power fan, independent of the cooling fan, with its own exhaust.
  4. Mosquitoes enter through the side grille; the funnel narrows towards the chamber to minimise escape.
  5. Holds captured mosquitoes. Slides out of the side for cleaning and disposal.

Battery run time, noise and mosquito capture effectiveness will be measured during prototype testing.

MosquitoGuard technology.

An active mosquito attraction and capture system. Instead of a mesh that only keeps mosquitoes out, MosquitoGuard draws them in and holds them in a removable chamber, all inside one integrated module on the side of the device.

How it works

  1. 1

    The scent lure and UV-A light attract mosquitoes towards the dedicated side intake.

  2. 2

    Mosquitoes approach the intake grille.

  3. 3

    A dedicated low-power suction fan pulls them inside.

  4. 4

    The one-way funnel guides them towards the collection chamber and minimises escape.

  5. 5

    Captured mosquitoes remain inside the removable mesh collection chamber.

  6. 6

    You remove the chamber periodically for cleaning and disposal.

Designed around

  • Separate airflowIts own suction fan and exhaust, behind a sealed partition. The cooling fan is never used for trapping.
  • Low powerA low-power UV-A LED and a small suction fan, to save battery for cooling.
  • Quiet runningSuction fan noise is a key design target for evenings and sleep.
  • Easy maintenanceA replaceable scent lure cartridge and a washable chamber, both reached from the side without taking the device apart.

MosquitoGuard is designed to reduce mosquitoes around your personal space. It won't attract every mosquito species or remove every mosquito: actual attraction and capture depend on the species, environmental conditions, lure formulation and prototype testing.

    Questions, answered honestly.

    Cooling, filters, MosquitoGuard, battery and price, answered plainly.

    What is Vayora?

    Vayora is a portable all-in-one personal climate comfort device. It combines personal air cooling, air purification, humidity management and MosquitoGuard active mosquito protection in one compact, rechargeable unit that you can use at home, at work, while travelling or outdoors.

    Is it an air conditioner?

    No. Vayora is a personal comfort device: it aims cooler, cleaner airflow at you rather than cooling a whole room. That's what keeps it small, easy to carry and able to run on a battery.

    How does MosquitoGuard work?

    A scent lure cartridge and a UV-A light attract mosquitoes towards an intake on the side of the device. A dedicated low-power suction fan pulls them in, and a one-way funnel guides them into a removable mesh collection chamber, where they stay until you empty it. Results depend on the mosquito species, the environment, the lure formulation and prototype testing, so it won't catch every mosquito.

    How do I empty the collection chamber?

    Slide the chamber out from the side of the device, empty it, wash it and slide it back. You don't need to take the device apart.

    How often does the scent lure cartridge need replacing?

    The cartridge is replaceable from the side compartment. Its replacement interval depends on the final lure formulation and will be confirmed after prototype testing.

    Does the mosquito airflow mix with the air I breathe?

    No. MosquitoGuard has its own suction fan and exhaust behind a sealed partition. The main cooling fan is never used for mosquito trapping, and the two airflows stay separate.

    How does the cooling work?

    Water from a small top-fill tank keeps a cooling pad damp. Filtered air passes through the pad and is cooled, and the front vents direct it towards you.

    How does the filtration work?

    Air passes through a washable pre-filter for larger dust, a HEPA-type filter for finer particles and an activated carbon filter for certain odours, before it is cooled.

    How does humidity management work?

    A sensor shows the humidity around you on the display. Comfort modes let you use plain airflow when it's already humid, and cooling with the damp pad when it's dry.

    How long does the battery last?

    Run time depends on the final battery capacity and on which functions you use, so it will be published once measured in prototype testing. MosquitoGuard uses a low-power UV-A LED and a small fan to save battery for cooling.

    Does it need installing?

    No. Set it on a table, desk or any flat surface, aim the vents and switch it on.
